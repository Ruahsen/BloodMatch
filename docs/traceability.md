> **ID conventions (read before using this matrix):**
> - **FR-01…FR-20** use the canonical IDs from `CONTEXT.md` §9.1 (an earlier revision of this table used a different numbering; it has been corrected here).

## 4. Business-Rule Verification (Q1 → Q25 Decisions from CONTEXT.md)

1. **Q1: Account Status Separation:** Verification status (`unverified`, `pending`, `verified`, `rejected`) is strictly decoupled from account status (`active`, `deactivated`). *(Implemented in `users` table; verified in Phase 3 & 5)*.
2. **Q2: Capability-Matrix Mapping:** Registration creates a `pending` active member capable of creating requests; verified status required for donor participation. *(Implemented in `AuthMiddleware.php` & `ProfileController.php`; verified in Phase 4 & 5)*.
3. **Q3: Document Uploads & Resubmissions:** Rejection allows uploading new documents and calling `POST /api/profile/resubmit` to re-enter pending triage. *(Implemented in `ProfileController.php`; verified in Phase 5)*.
4. **Q4: Age Eligibility Rules:** 16–17 allowed with parental consent document; <16 restricted; >=18 standard. *(Implemented in `VerificationService.php`; verified in Phase 5)*.
5. **Q5: Red-Cell Compatibility Priority:** Biological compatibility is strictly non-negotiable and precedes proximity ranking. *(Implemented in `MatchService.php` + `BloodCompatibilityService.php`; verified in Phase 7)*.
6. **Q6: Matching Generation & Material Re-matching:** Matches are tagged with generation numbers; material request changes increment generation to alert donors. *(Implemented in `MatchService.php`; verified in Phase 7 & 10; donor-location refresh explicitly without bump — L20)*.
7. **Q7: Explicit Volunteer Donor Enrollment:** Verified members must explicitly opt in via `POST /api/profile/enroll-donor`. *(Implemented in `ProfileController.php`; verified in Phase 8)*.
8. **Q8: Chapter Ranking vs Geographic Fallback:** Donors in the same chapter are ranked before external chapters; backend-resolved reference coordinates provide distance ranking within chapter. *(Implemented in `MatchService.php` + `Geo.php`; verified in Phase 7 & Location L17–L19)*.
9. **Q9: Geographic Coordinates Anonymization:** Exact requester/donor coordinates are suppressed in match responses (`approximate_distance_km` only; selector APIs expose none). *(Implemented in `MatchService::privacySafeMatches` + `LocationService`; verified in Phase 7 & Location L13/L16)*.
10. **Q10: Urgency Levels:** Three standard levels (`routine`, `urgent`, `critical`) with validation on request creation. *(Implemented in `RequestService.php`; verified in Phase 6)*.
11. **Q11: In-App Notification Deduplication:** `(dedup_key, generation)` unique compound key prevents notification spam while enabling re-match alerts. *(Implemented in `NotificationService.php`; verified in Phase 10)*.
12. **Q12: Email Notification Transport:** PHPMailer transport with rate-limiting and critical urgency bypass; failures logged without aborting transactions. *(Implemented in `Mailer.php`; verified in Phase 10)*.
13. **Q13: Privacy on Regional Demand Map:** Public map displays canonical chapter centroids only; no individual requester pins or identities. *(Implemented in `Analytics/DemandMapController.php`; verified in Phase 12)*.
14. **Q14: Donation Confirmation Transaction:** Multi-entity atomic transaction (`FOR UPDATE`) executing report confirmation, match completion, and fulfillment check. *(Implemented in `DonationService.php`; verified in Phase 8)*.
15. **Q15: 42-Hour Standby Window:** Derived dynamically from `last_verified_donation_at`; donor availability changes locked during window. *(Implemented in `DonorEligibilityService.php`; verified in Phase 9)*.
16. **Q16: 90-Day Cooldown Interval:** Standard administrative recovery interval derived from `last_verified_donation_at`. *(Implemented in `DonorEligibilityService.php`; verified in Phase 9)*.
17. **Q17: Request Lifecycle Invariants:** Requests transition linearly through `OPEN` → `FULFILLED`, `CANCELLED`, or `EXPIRED`. Active `OPEN` requests are excluded from resolution denominators. *(Implemented in `RequestService.php` & `Repositories/AnalyticsRepository.php`; verified in Phase 6 & 12)*.
18. **Q18: Donor Availability Toggles:** Donors can toggle availability freely unless locked by active Standby or Cooldown windows. *(Implemented in `ProfileController.php`; verified in Phase 8 & 9)*.
19. **Q19: Request Fulfillment Threshold:** Request automatically marks `FULFILLED` when completed donation count equals `quantity_units`. *(Implemented in `DonationService.php`; verified in Phase 8)*.
20. **Q20: Audit Logging Immobility:** `audit_log` records are append-only; database triggers reject UPDATE and DELETE operations. *(Implemented in Migration 004 triggers; verified in Phase 11 & 16)*.
21. **Q21: Fixed 3-Chapter Geographic Scope:** Fixed chapters seeded as `mt_samat`, `mt_tarak`, and `meridian_heights`. Display convention `{name} ({municipality})`. *(Implemented in Migration 001 & Seed 001; verified in Phase 4 & 12)*.
22. **Q22: Chapter Officer Authority & Isolation:** Officers can only review, confirm, and audit within their assigned chapter. *(Implemented in `Middleware/AuthMiddleware.php`; verified in Phase 4, 11, 12)*.
23. **Q23: Blood Type Provenance Upgrade:** Officer review of physical donor card upgrades blood type from self-reported to officer-verified. *(Implemented in `VerificationService.php`; verified in Phase 5)*.
24. **Q24: Soft Deactivation State:** Deactivating an account halts active sessions and donor matching without destroying historical donation records. *(Implemented in `AuthService.php` & `MatchService.php`; verified in Phase 3 & 4)*.
25. **Q25: Single-Use Password Reset Tokens:** Cryptographically secure 64-hex SHA-256 tokens expiring in 30 minutes and invalidated upon first use. *(Implemented in `AuthService.php`; verified in Phase 3)*.
